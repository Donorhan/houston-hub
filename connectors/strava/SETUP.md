# Connect Strava

1. Choose **Read-only** or **Read and write** and select **Authorize connection**.
2. Sign in to Strava if asked. Strava lists the permissions Houston asks for: keep every box
   ticked and select **Authorize**.

> If you untick a permission, Houston cannot finish connecting: authorize again with every box
> ticked. Switching a connection to **Read and write** asks Strava for the extra permissions.

## Advanced

### Strava says the app has reached its athlete limit

Strava limits how many athletes can connect Houston's Strava app until Strava reviews it. Try
again later.

### Revoke access

In Strava, open **Settings** > [**My Apps**](https://www.strava.com/settings/apps) and select
**Revoke Access** next to Houston, then remove the connection in Houston.
