# Strava

Let agents look at your training: your activities with their laps, splits, heart rate, power
and GPS data, your weekly and yearly totals, your training zones, and the comments and kudos on
your activities. Agents can also read your clubs, bikes and shoes, routes (and download them as
GPX or TCX files), starred segments and your efforts on them.

**Read** is the default. **Write** adds logging manual activities, importing activity
files from your devices, editing an activity's title, description, sport, gear and tags, updating
your weight and starring segments.

> A Strava connection is personal: only the person who connected it can use it. It includes
> activities you keep private (**Only You**). Some data, such as segment efforts and activity
> zones, needs a Strava subscription.

Strava does not let apps delete activities, read other athletes' profiles, club members or club
activities, and it limits how many requests Houston can make across all its Strava users.
