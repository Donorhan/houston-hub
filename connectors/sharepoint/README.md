# Microsoft SharePoint

Help agents find what your team keeps in SharePoint: documents in team sites,
the lists that track projects and requests, and intranet pages.

- Search every site you can open, OneDrive files shared with you included,
  and find sites by name or address.
- Browse document libraries, download files, or convert Word, Excel and
  PowerPoint documents to PDF.
- Read lists with their columns and items, and read modern pages.
- With **Write** access: upload files up to 250 MB, create folders, rename,
  move, copy and delete files, and add, change and delete list items.

Works with work or school accounts; most organizations need an administrator
to approve Houston SharePoint once. A connection reaches what you can open in
SharePoint, never more. Read access cannot change or delete anything, and
deleted files go to the site's recycle bin.
