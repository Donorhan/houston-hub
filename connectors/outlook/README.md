# Microsoft Outlook

Give agents the context they need from your Outlook mailbox.

- Search mail with Outlook's search syntax or filters, and read whole conversations.
- Download attachments to work with alongside your other files.
- With **Draft** access: prepare drafts, replies and forwards (with
  attachments) for you to review and send from Outlook.
- With **Write** access: also send, reply and forward, mark messages read or
  unread, flag and categorize them, and organize mail into folders.

Works with Outlook.com and Microsoft 365 work or school mailboxes. Read access
cannot send mail or change your mailbox. Draft access can create, update and
delete drafts, including ones you wrote, but Houston refuses every request to
send. Deleted messages stay recoverable in Outlook for your mailbox's retention
period.
