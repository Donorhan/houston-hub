# Connect your Google account

1. Choose the level for **Access**:
   - **Read** to let agents search, read, download and export your files in My
     Drive and shared drives, and see who they are shared with.
   - **Write** to also let agents create folders and files, upload files and new
     versions, rename, move and copy them, share them and remove access, move
     them to the trash and delete them permanently.
2. Choose an available sign-in option, choose your account, and approve the
   permissions Google lists. With Connect with Google, if Google shows a
   checkbox per permission, tick them all: Houston refuses a partial grant.

Houston calls Google Drive only when your agent asks it to and never syncs your
files in the background. Connect with Google asks only for the permissions of
the access level you choose, so connecting Google Drive does not grant access to
your other Google services.

> If you connect with Composio, it requires full Google Drive access. Houston
> limits its use to the access level you select.

## Advanced: changing access and troubleshooting

- To change the access level, edit the connection and connect the same account
  again. With Connect with Google, Google then asks for the new set of
  permissions.
- To revoke access, remove the connection in Houston, or remove Composio (or
  Houston for direct sign-in) from
  [your Google Account's third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
  This also affects other connections using that app.
- Google Workspace administrators can block third-party apps. If Google refuses
  the connection, ask your administrator to allow the app shown on Google's
  consent screen.
