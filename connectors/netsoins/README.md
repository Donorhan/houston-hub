# NETSoins

Connect a NETSoins facility so your agents can list its staff accounts: who has
access, their job category and permission profiles, professional identifiers such
as RPPS, whether the account is active, when it expires and when it was last used.
For example, they can review accounts before an audit or spot leavers whose access
is still open.

Staff records also hold personal details: birth date and place, home address,
phone numbers and email addresses. Agents with this connection can read them.

Access is read-only. Agents cannot create, change or deactivate accounts.

> Resident records and care data stay out of reach: Houston only lets this
> connection read staff accounts and job categories.

Each NETSoins facility has its own address and API key, so connect each facility
separately.
